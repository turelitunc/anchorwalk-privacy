Privacy policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Anchorwalk records, why, and what happens to it. In short: your records stay only on this phone; the app never sends them anywhere, shares them with anyone or sells them.

Who is responsible, and how to reach them

The developer of the app, and the person responsible for this policy: Tunç Türeli.

For your questions and requests: anchorwalk.support@gmail.com.

Your records never reach the developer; the developer cannot see, keep or process them.

If you send feedback, your email app sends what you wrote, with the app version, language and Android version, to the developer's address; nothing from your records is added. The app keeps a short list of its own errors on the phone: when each happened, its type, a short description and where in the app's code it came up; your records and song titles never go in it. This list, with when and why Android closed the app, goes into a feedback email only if you tick "Add technical details", and you can see what will be sent first.

Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to answer them; write to the address above to have yours deleted.

What is recorded

While tracking is on, even when the app is not open, a summary of the following is recorded every minute (raw sensor data is not kept):

  • Motion: from the phone's motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, gravity sensor), the number and angle of turns; measures of the turns' pattern (how many of them go back and forth, how straight the back-and-forth line stays, how evenly spaced the turns are); and measures of rocking and of how strong the movement is.
  • Steps: from the phone's step counter. For this, Android's "Physical activity" permission is requested.
  • Music and headphones: whether anything is playing, the media volume level while it plays, whether headphones are connected, and their type (wired, Bluetooth or USB); if music plays on a Bluetooth speaker or in a car, that too.
  • What is playing, only if you gave "Notification access": the title, artist and length of what is playing (a song, video or podcast), the playback position, and the app playing it.
  • The phone's state: whether the screen is on, whether the proximity sensor is covered (for example, while the phone is in a pocket) and whether a call is going on. Who called you or whom you called is not recorded.
  • App time, only if you gave "Usage access": whether the apps on your "App limits" list (Instagram and X by default) are open on the screen. With this permission Android gives the usage records of all apps; the app skips all but the ones on that list and does not keep them. When you add an app, the list of apps on the phone is only shown on the screen where you pick one, and is not saved; the name, package name and time limit of the ones you chose are saved. A file you import brings its own list, which replaces yours. The past minutes of an app you take off the list stay recorded until "Delete all".

The motion sensors run while music plays, on headphones or on the phone's speaker. If "Count pacing without music too" is on in "Detection", they also run when no music is playing: they switch on for about 45 seconds when you start moving or turn on the screen, also when the app is closed and the screen is off, and again from time to time while the screen stays on; if it looks like pacing, they stay on, and each minute's turns, steps and how regular the turns are get recorded as above. That time counts in the day's total; a "No, I'm not" on a reminder takes it out. The step counter runs whenever tracking is on.

Besides these, the following is also recorded:

  • What you enter: the times you saved with "Mark", your answers to reminders and long-session breaks, the time you noted daydreaming without moving on a day (from the evening question or the home screen), your daily goal, your settings and your focus sessions (plan, start and end times, MD time in each block).
  • Tracking history: technical records such as when tracking was turned on and off, when reminders and long-session breaks came and how they ended, and when and why Android closed the app. It is kept to find the times when tracking was interrupted.
  • Your audio devices: the names of the Bluetooth audio devices music plays on, the end of each one's Bluetooth address (to tell two of the same model apart), the day music last played on each, and whether each is headphones, a speaker or a car. They are kept to decide whether a reminder's short sound may play and which rule counts pacing; the app tells from a device's name or asks you. You can see and change them in "Your audio devices" in "Detection"; no extra permission is needed for this. They stay on this phone: they are not in the file "My data" exports, and "Delete all" deletes them too.

The app does not access your location, microphone, camera, contacts, messages or photos.

It does not read other apps' notifications. When Do Not Disturb is on, it connects to notifications for about a second to see whether its own reminder or long-session break was silenced; Android passes on other notifications during that time too, and the app does not open, read or keep them.

What it is used for

This information is used only to:

  • recognize pacing and rocking to music, on headphones or the phone's speaker, and pacing without music if "Count pacing without music too" is on;
  • show you how long it lasts each day, at what times, and which tracks went with it;
  • if the evening question is on, ask each evening at the time set, and show the time you note apart from the measured time;
  • remind you while it is going on, and pause the music playing when you tap "Pause music for 5 min", or on its own once "Long-session break" is due (at the latest after the time set there, 30 minutes unless you change it); if music starts again during those 5 minutes, it is paused again: up to 3 times after "Pause music for 5 min", every time during the long-session break;
  • bring reminders and the long-session break sooner, and reminders a level stronger, when the music is loud or you pace faster than usual (your usual pace is worked out from your own records);
  • run focus sessions and suggest a plan from your own records;
  • show the time spent in the apps on your "App limits" list, and let you know when you pass the limit.

It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is kept

All records are kept only on this phone, in the app's own storage. You do not need to create an account.

The app has no internet permission; no record is sent to a server. Records are not included in the phone's cloud backup or in transfers to a new phone either.

Sharing, advertising and analytics

Your records are not shared with anyone or sold. The app has no advertising, analytics or automatic crash reporting tools.

Updates

To keep you on a current version, the app asks the Google Play app on your phone whether a newer version exists, telling it only the app's name and version number and the version of Google's update library. Google Play does the checking, downloading and installing under Google's own terms and privacy policy; to check, Google says it uses device details, the app's version and its installed modules. The app has no internet permission and sends nothing over the internet; your records are not involved.

For this the app keeps a few settings on the phone: which version's "What's new" notes it last showed, until when "Later" hides the "New version available" card on the home screen, and whether a required update was started or could not be finished.

How long it is kept

Records stay on the phone until you delete them; the app does not delete old records on its own. When you uninstall the app, Android deletes all of the app's records.

A file you export goes where you choose; from then on, it is in your hands.

Your rights and choices

From "My data" in the home screen's menu you can export your records to a file (all of them but your audio devices), restore a file you exported, or delete all your records. In "Raw data" you can see the latest minutes, today's marks, the time you wrote down as spent without moving and the tracking history.

You can turn tracking off at any time, and turn off permissions you gave in the phone's settings. If you do not give notification access or usage access, tracking still works, but song titles and app times won't be shown. Counting pacing without music and the evening question stay off unless you turn them on in "Detection" or import a file that has them on, and you can turn them off in "Detection" at any time.

Age

The app is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current text is published in the app ("About" > "Privacy policy") and the effective date is updated. If an update changes what information is recorded or where it goes, the app also says so separately.
