Anchorwalk support

Anchorwalk is made by one person, Tunç Türeli. If something doesn't work as it should, or you have a question or an idea, write to me.

Contact

Email: anchorwalk.support@gmail.com

The easiest way is from the app: open the menu, then "About" › "Feedback and suggestions", write your message and tap "Send". Your email app opens with it. The app adds its version, its language and your phone's system version (on iPhone, also the device model), and its own recent errors only if you tick "Add technical details". Nothing from your records is added.

If you write from somewhere else, please include your phone model, its Android or iOS version, the app version (at the bottom of "About") and what happened.

I read every message.

Common questions

Where are my records kept?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no server, so I can't restore anything for you, and the records aren't in your phone's backups either. Export them before you change or reset your phone.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, use "My data" › "Export" and save the file somewhere the new phone can reach. On the new phone, install Anchorwalk and use "My data" › "Import". This works between Android and iPhone in both directions. Importing deletes the data on the new phone and puts what is in the file in its place.

The evening question or focus session alerts don't arrive

First check that notifications are allowed for Anchorwalk in your phone's settings.

  • iPhone: the evening question is an ordinary notification, so a Focus mode such as Do Not Disturb can silence it; you can add Anchorwalk to the apps that Focus allows. The focus session alerts are Time Sensitive, so they can still come through a Focus, unless you turned off "Time Sensitive Notifications" in Settings › Notifications › Anchorwalk.
  • Android: if Android restricts Anchorwalk in the background, the evening question can come late; set the app's battery use to unrestricted (on Samsung: Settings › Apps › Anchorwalk › Battery). For focus session alerts on time, allow "Alarms & reminders"; while it is off, the "Focus session" screen offers to take you there.

How do I change the app's language?

  • iPhone: Settings › Apps › Anchorwalk › Language (on iOS 16 and 17, Settings › Anchorwalk › Language). If Language isn't there, first add another language in Settings › General › Language & Region.
  • Android 13 and later: in the app, "About" › "Language". On earlier versions the app follows the phone's language.

How do I delete everything?

In "My data" › "Delete all", tap "Delete all data" and confirm: every record and setting on the phone is deleted; it can't be undone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything too. Files you exported are not deleted; delete them yourself if you no longer want them.

Does the iPhone app notice pacing on its own?

No. On iPhone, Anchorwalk doesn't use the motion sensors: when you catch yourself in a daydream, tap "Mark" and save the time, or answer the evening question for the whole day. Only the Android app notices pacing and rocking on its own.

Not a medical device

Anchorwalk is not a medical device; it does not diagnose or treat any condition. If daydreaming gets in the way of your daily life, talking with a psychologist or psychiatrist may help. If you are in danger or need urgent help, contact your local emergency services.
